THE NEZĀM CAMPAIGN

Route. Ride. Right.

A better journey starts with better information, safer choices and respect for the people sharing the road with you.

Why does this matter?

Small sources of disorder create a harder journey for everyone.

👥 Overcrowding

Too many passengers means less comfort and higher risk.

⏱️ Delays

Unpredictable stops and traffic make arrival uncertain.

🚏 Random Stops

Unplanned stops disrupt routes and increase disorder.

🏎️ Unsafe Driving

Speeding and harsh driving can put passengers at risk.

🎟️ Ticket Confusion

Outdated information can create disputes at boarding.

📊 Data Gaps

Better decisions need timely, reliable information.

Ride Right — Know the Rules

Simple habits that make public transport safer and more organized.

🚌 Before boarding

🟢 Wait at the official stop.
🟢 Let passengers exit before boarding.
🟢 Have your ticket ready.

🚍 While boarding

🟢 Don't push or rush.
🟢 Keep doors and aisles clear.
🟢 Hold the handrail when needed.

❤️ Respect the ride

🟢 Give priority seats to people who need them.
🟢 Keep the vehicle clean.
🟢 Respect every passenger.

🚫 Don't

🚫 Don't distract the driver.
🚫 Don't block doors or emergency equipment.
🚫 Don't board outside official stops.

When something goes wrong

Know the right action instead of reacting blindly.

🩺 Medical

Stay calm, alert the driver and use Emergency Assistance when necessary.

🛡️ Harassment

Move to a safer area if possible and use Silent SOS or Report.

🏎️ Unsafe driving

Don't confront the driver. Report the issue with route and trip details.
